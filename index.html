WHAT GUIDES MY WORK

I studied engineering at Netaji Subhas Institute of Technology (NSIT). Along the way, coding became more than a subject — it became something I wanted to help other people get comfortable with.

At Coding Blocks, I work across teaching, academic planning and curriculum. A lot has changed since we started in 2014, but what I enjoy most hasn't: explaining a tricky idea, writing some code together and seeing students gain confidence.

I'm also involved in shaping the learning experience at Coding Blocks School of Technology, where the focus is on strong CS fundamentals, hands-on development and learning how to work with AI thoughtfully.

03 / HOW I APPROACH LEARNING

A few things I
keep coming back to.

01 ↗

Start with the 'why'.

Syntax matters, but understanding the idea behind a solution stays with you much longer.

02 ↗
> build something_✓ learnt by doing

Write the code yourself.

Watching is a start. Building, debugging and trying again is where the real learning happens.

03 ↗
AI+CS

Use AI. Keep the fundamentals.

Tools are getting better every day. Knowing how to think through a problem is still your biggest advantage.
