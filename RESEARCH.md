## Editorial choices

- First-person site prose is drafted and **requires Kartik's approval** before public release; it should not be presented as an actual transcript or verified quotation.
- The text deliberately avoids fabricated internships, company roles, degrees beyond B.Tech, student testimonials or unverified placement claims.
- The one large numeric reach figure is explicitly tied to the Coding Blocks organisation. This avoids unintentionally implying an individual figure of 2.5 lakh students taught.
